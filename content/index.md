# hi! i'm xavier.

i'm a PhD student in the [Laboratory for Social Cognitive Science](https://www.cushmanlab.fas.harvard.edu) at Harvard, led by Fiery Cushman. i work on people and on AI.

in human psychology, i study how culture scaffolds cognition, enabling people to solve tasks more complex than anyone can hope to achieve on their own. i also work on contractualist moral psychology and various methods topics.

in AI security, i study the human data problem for scalable oversight: how do we encode what we want, and design human-AI interactions that ensure AI systems robustly do that? i have also done work on technical governance topics (e.g., evals methods & incident reporting systems).

previously, i read psych & philosophy at new college, oxford (studying empathy and deep learning theory). i then worked at mckinsey's london office on strategy, org design, and data/analytics topics.

i write at [seeing truly](https://seeingtruly.substack.com/) on substack.
